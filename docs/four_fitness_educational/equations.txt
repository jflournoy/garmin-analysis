FOUR-FITNESS STATE-SPACE MODEL EQUATIONS
==================================================

Impulse dynamics:
I_i[t] = ψ_i I_i[t-1] + X_i[t]

Fitness dynamics:
F_i[t] = α_i F_i[t-1] + β_i I_i[t-1]

Observation equation:
W[t] = Σ_i γ_i F_i[t] + GP(t) + f_daily(t) + ε_w[t]

Complete prediction:
Ŵ[t] = Σ_i γ_i Σ_{k=1}^t α_i^{k-1} β_i Σ_{j=0}^{t-k} ψ_i^j X_i[t-k-j]

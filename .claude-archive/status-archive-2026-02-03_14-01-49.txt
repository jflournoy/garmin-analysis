## Addressing LOO Overhead (User Question)

**LOO-CV computational overhead**:
- LOO requires computing leave-one-out predictive densities for each observation (O(N))
- For N=147 observations in current dataset, adds ~147x additional computation
- WAIC is faster and often more robust to high Pareto k values
- **Recommendation**: Use WAIC for model comparison when speed is important or LOO shows high k values

**Practical steps**:
1. Use `check_loo_reliability()` to assess if LOO is reliable
2. If unreliable or slow, use WAIC via `compare_models_with_fallback()`
3. Examine high-k observations with `get_high_k_observations()`
4. Consider model modifications for robustness

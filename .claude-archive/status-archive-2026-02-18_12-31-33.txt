## Fix Double-Detrending in Spline Decomposition Visualization - IN PROGRESS - 2026-02-11 17:06:19

**Task**: Fix double-detrending issue where detrended observations in spline decomposition plot were incorrectly negative due to adding y_mean multiple times.

**Root Cause**: The back-transform function added y_mean to each component (fitness effect, GP trend, daily spline) individually, causing double subtraction when computing detrended observations.

**Changes Made**:
1. **plot_cyclic.py**: Split `back_transform` into `back_transform_component` (no y_mean) and `back_transform_total` (adds y_mean). Applied to `plot_state_space_spline_decomposition` and `plot_state_space_component_details`.
2. **Detrended observations calculation**: Subtract y_mean once: `detrended_obs = obs_weight - fitness_mean_bt - gp_mean_bt - y_mean`.

**Files Modified**:
- `src/models/plot_cyclic.py`: Lines 1758-1767, 1779, 2054-2065

**Next Steps**: Regenerate visualizations to verify fix.

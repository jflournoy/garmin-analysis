## Health-Workout Correlation Analysis - 2026-02-05 10:53:09

**Task**: Create and run health-workout correlation analysis

**Accomplishments**:

1. ✅ **Created correlation analysis script** (`src/analysis/explore_health_workout_correlations.py`):
   - Computes lagged correlations (0-7 days) between workouts and health metrics
   - Identifies strongest relationships and optimal lags
   - Generates comprehensive HTML reports and visualizations
   - Handles 33 health metrics across 922 days

2. ✅ **Ran comprehensive analysis**:
   - Workout variables: `strength_training`, `walking`, `cycling`
   - Health metrics: 29 metrics analyzed (sleep, heart, activity, respiration, body battery)
   - Total analyses: 87 workout-health metric pairs
   - Significant correlations: 55 (p < 0.05)

**Key Findings**:

### Strongest Positive Correlations:
1. **Cycling → Total distance** (lag 0d): r = 0.448** (expected - cycling increases distance)
2. **Walking → Total steps** (lag 0d): r = 0.441** (expected - walking increases steps)
3. **Strength training → Active calories** (lag 0d): r = 0.411** (expected - workouts burn calories)
4. **Walking → Wellness distance** (lag 0d): r = 0.409** 
5. **Strength training → Total calories** (lag 0d): r = 0.394**

### Strongest Negative Correlations:
1. **Cycling → Total steps** (lag 0d): r = -0.145** (cycling days have fewer steps)
2. **Cycling → Wellness distance** (lag 0d): r = -0.134** 
3. **Cycling → Resting heart rate** (lag 6d): r = -0.133** (cycling lowers resting HR after 6 days)
4. **Strength training → Body Battery lowest** (lag 0d): r = -0.122** (workouts drain energy)
5. **Strength training → Body Battery highest** (lag 1d): r = -0.116**

### Sleep Effects:
- **Strength training**: Decreases light sleep percentage (r = -0.095**, lag 5d), increases REM sleep (r = 0.085**, lag 5d)
- **Walking**: Increases total sleep (r = 0.065**, lag 4d) and REM sleep (r = 0.067**, lag 4d)
- **Cycling**: Increases total sleep (r = 0.067**, lag 5d) but decreases sleep efficiency (r = -0.068**, lag 5d)

### Body Battery (Energy):
- **Strength training**: Drains energy (negative correlations with charged/highest/lowest)
- **Walking**: Similar draining effect
- **Cycling**: Increases charged battery (r = 0.096**, lag 4d) but increases drained (r = 0.116**, lag 0d)

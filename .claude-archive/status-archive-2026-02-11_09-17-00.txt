**Task**: Update state-space model to use weakly informative priors and initialize at prior centers

**Changes Made**:

1. **Updated Stan model priors** ([stan/weight_state_space.stan](stan/weight_state_space.stan)):
   - Changed `sigma_f` and `sigma_w` priors from `std_normal()` to `exponential(1)`
   - More flexible for standardized data (mean=1, mode=0 vs half-normal mean=0.798)

2. **Updated initialization** ([src/models/fit_weight.py](src/models/fit_weight.py)):
   - `alpha`: 0.5 (beta(2,2) prior mean/mode)
   - `beta`: 0.0 (normal(0,1) prior mean/mode)
   - `gamma`: 0.0 (normal(0,1) prior mean/mode)
   - `sigma_f`, `sigma_w`: 0.5 (exponential(1) - starting smaller for stability)
   - `alpha_gp`: 0.5 (normal(0,1) truncated [0.01,5] - reasonable value)
   - `rho_gp`: 0.25 (inv_gamma(3,0.5) prior mean)

3. **Testing**:
   - Verified model compiles and runs with synthetic data
   - Tests pass with minimal sampling settings
   - Fixed initialization issue with `alpha_gp` at lower bound (0.01 → 0.5)

**Rationale**:
- Weakly informative priors provide regularization without being overly restrictive
- Initialization at prior centers helps MCMC convergence
- Exponential(1) priors for noise are more flexible than half-normal(1) for standardized data
- Avoiding boundary initialization (e.g., `alpha_gp` at 0.01) improves sampler stability

**Current Status**:

   - Models fitness state evolution: `fitness[t] = α·fitness[t-1] + β·intensity[t-1] + ε_f[t]`
